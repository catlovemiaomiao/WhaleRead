The Harbor Letter

Mara carried the letter in her pocket as the ferry crossed the harbor. The paper was warm from her hand, although the morning wind was cold.

At the pier, Iven held the door for a woman with a blue suitcase. A gull landed on the railing and watched them without a sound.

The letter was burning a hole in Mara's pocket. She wanted to read it again, but she waited until she reached the quiet room above the bakery.

There she opened the envelope. Inside was a drawing of the old lighthouse, with a small cross beside the northern window.

By noon, the fog had lifted. Mara could see the lighthouse from the desk, and for the first time she understood why the drawing contained no door.
